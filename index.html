RULES & SYSTEM FEATURES

ID Card Generator System

⚡ Main Capabilities

  • Inline Direct Editing: Click directly on any text inside the card preview to modify contents instantly.
  • Batch Processing: Expand "Batch Processing" in the sidebar to apply training dates, instructor names, or company details to all generated cards at once.
  • Print Layout Engine: Optimized A4 rendering fits exactly 3 ID cards per printed page without page bleed or unwanted margins.
  • Data Continuity: Automatically saves all work into your browser's LocalStorage.

🛠️ Data Backup & Management

  • Export: Download a full `.json` backup of all active card records.
  • Import: Restore or load prepared card sets from a JSON backup.
  • Search Filter: Instant filter by name, employer, or card ID.

🚨 Troubleshooting & Common Fixes

Q: Cards overlap or overflow during printing?

A: Set your browser print dialog margins to "None" or "Default" and scale to 100%.

Q: Changes are not saving?

A: Ensure you are not in Private/Incognito mode with restricted LocalStorage permissions.
